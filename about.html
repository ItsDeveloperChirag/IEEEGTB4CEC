About IEEE GTB4CEC Student Branch

Learn about our history, mission, and vision

Our History

The IEEE GTB4CEC Student Branch was established to provide students at Guru Tegh Bahadur 4th Centenary Engineering College with opportunities to enhance their technical and professional skills.

Our Mission

Our mission is to transform students into tech leaders of tomorrow by fostering a culture of continuous innovation, technical integrity, and community-driven development at GTB4CEC.

Our Vision

To be a leading student branch that inspires and empowers the next generation of engineers and technologists to create innovative solutions for global challenges.

What We Do

Educational Programs

Workshops, seminars, and training sessions to enhance technical knowledge and skills in emerging technologies.

Networking Events

Connect with industry professionals, alumni, and fellow students to build lasting professional relationships.

Competitions

Technical competitions, hackathons, and project showcases to promote innovation and excellence.
